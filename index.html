Community Initiatives

Our awareness initiatives use music as a platform to promote understanding of the connection between music, the brain, and overall wellbeing — through community-based musical experiences, outreach, and opportunities to engage with the role music plays in memory, emotion, and human connection.

TAP Talks

Music and the brain, made accessible

TAP Talks are short presentations on music and the brain, delivered at libraries, community centers, and public events. Topics we cover include:

  1. How music affects memory in Alzheimer's patients
  2. Music and Memory: How Songs Shape the Way We Remember
  3. The Brain's Reward System: Why Music Feels Good

Chapter Relations

Growing across ages and places

As we grow, we are expanding our mission to different age groups and locations. Here are our current active chapters.

High School

High School Chapter

Exploring the connection between music, the mind, and community impact through leadership, education, and outreach.

  • Healthcare Performances: Live acoustic sets at healthcare facilities to foster connection and well-being.
  • Music & Brain Insights: Exploring music, neuroscience, and mental well-being.
  • Community Outreach: Initiatives that use music for awareness, storytelling, and connection.
  • Music & Mind Campaigns: Educational content on how music influences emotion, memory, and cognition.
  • Student Leadership: Empowering students to lead projects and expand TAP's mission.
Middle School

Middle School Chapter

Focused on music creation, storytelling, and community outreach for a younger age group.

  • Sound Map: Turning classmate feelings into acoustic pieces.
  • Music & Experiments: Exploring tempo, dynamics, and emotions.
  • Mini Picture Books: Song-inspired stories for hospital patients.
  • Music & Mind Cards: Calming cards with acoustic QR codes.
  • Song of the Month: A newsletter bridging art, story, and sound.
  • Audio Series: Live library performances and podcasts.

Interested in starting a chapter?

Requirements: musical passion, dedication to community outreach, and an interest in bridging cognitive science with sound. Questions? theacousticproject.sd@gmail.com

About Us

Our theme & mission

The Acoustic Project believes in the profound connection between music and the human mind.

Our work is about intentional connection. We believe in the power of music to unite communities, enrich well-being, and deepen our understanding of the human experience.
