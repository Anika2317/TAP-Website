Who we are

A student-led initiative with a musical background and an interest in showcasing the bridge between music and the mind.

What we do

We perform live acoustic sets, host public awareness events, and curate a digital journal of musical memories shared directly by our community.

TAP Services

What TAP actually does in the community.

Performances

Recurring live acoustic performances to communities that support the mind and well-being across San Diego.

Initiatives

Awareness initiatives that use music to encourage conversations about brain health, memory, and wellbeing through outreach, musical experiences, and education.

TAP Talks

Interactive, accessible presentations on music and the brain delivered at libraries and community centers.

Dedications

Community members submit a song and the memory behind it. We showcase these moments to share what they mean to our community.

TAP Journal

Our journal exploring the intersection of music, neuroscience, and human experience: research insights and reflections on memory, emotion, and cognition.

Community initiatives

Our awareness initiatives focus on using music as a platform to promote understanding of the connection between music, the brain, and overall wellbeing. These may include community-based musical experiences, outreach efforts, and opportunities to engage with the role music plays in memory, emotion, and human connection.

Chapter Relations

As we grow, we are expanding our mission to different age groups and locations. Here are our current active chapters.

High School Chapter

Focused on exploring the connection between music, the mind, and community impact through leadership, education, and outreach.

  • Healthcare Performances Bringing live acoustic sets to healthcare facilities to foster connection and well-being.
  • Music & Brain Insights Exploring the relationship between music, neuroscience, and mental well-being.
  • Community Outreach Initiatives that use music to create awareness, storytelling, and meaningful connections.
  • Music & Mind Campaigns Educational content about how music influences emotions, memory, and cognition.
  • Student Leadership Empowering students to lead projects and expand TAP's mission within their communities.

Middle School Chapter

Focused on music creation, storytelling, and community outreach for a younger age group.

  • Sound Map Turning classmate feelings into acoustic pieces.
  • Music & Experiments Exploring tempo, dynamics, and emotions.
  • Mini Picture Books Song-inspired stories for hospital patients.
  • Music & Mind Cards Calming cards with acoustic QR codes.
  • Song of the Month A newsletter bridging art, story, and sound.
  • Audio Series Live library performances and podcasts.

Our Theme & Mission

The Acoustic Project believes in the profound connection between music and the human mind.

Our work is about intentional connection. We believe in the power of music to unite communities, enrich well-being, and deepen our understanding of the human experience.
