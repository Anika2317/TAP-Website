The Concept
Why we are doing this.
TAP has been playing monthly indoor sessions at care facilities and hospital units. Those gigs are great, but they happen behind closed doors. This pop-up is our first public footprint where anyone passing by can see what the project does, interact with us, and help build our network.
The Setup
How it works.
Live Music
A casual 45-minute acoustic set along Mission Bay Drive. We will use a portable Bluetooth speaker to make sure the sound carries naturally. No massive gear, just a clean setup.
The Community Clipboard
We will set up a small folding table with a dedicated clipboard. Passersby can write down songs that hold a deep personal memory for them. We will take these community requests and use them to build our future performance setlists at hospitals and senior centers.

Clipboard Prompts
Ideas for people who stop by.
To help people figure out what to write down, we can suggest a few simple angles:
"What is a classic song you are absolute certain you will never forget?"
"What song instantly reminds you of a specific person from your past?"
"What track brings back an exact moment or a specific summer?"
"What melody always makes you stop what you are doing and just listen?"
